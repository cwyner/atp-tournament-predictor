Here is a mock-up workflow of how this project should go and what I want it to do:

############################################################################################################
1: Create scraping scripts to take data from ATP Tour website.

They provide very detailed data for three important categories:
- Serving
- Returning
- Under Pressure

Also gather some data about match outcomes, rankings, H2H?

I also want to have separarate stats for surface type (clay vs. grass vs. hard-court) since this is very important for players.

############################################################################################################
2: I want the model to combine the individual player statistics along with their match results to find out which stats mean the most.
This will also help us determine which players are the best.

This part is the training bit, I suppose. This part will probably require the most research on how to do.
Will likely use PyTorch or something to do this part.

############################################################################################################
3: My ultimate goal is to simulate a bracket. If I have the matchups as inputs, I need to make sure the model understands such an input and
can use it to output a winner.

Here is where the mismatch is in my mind:

Training fields (with supervision):
Serve Rating    1st Serve %     1st Serve pts won   2nd Serve pts won   ... ... (now match statistics)  Opponent    Result

Testing fields
Player1     Player2     Result

I will need to do a lot of thinking on how these pieces fit together in my head.
Preliminary thinking at a high level: 

The player data on its own doesn't mean much. I need to compare a player's serve rating to how many wins they have, return rating
to how many wins they have, etc. Then, maybe I can redesign the fields to look more like this:

Serve Rating Player 1    1st Serve % Player 1   ... Serve Rating Player 2    1st Serve % Player 2   ... Result

I think this makes much more sense. This way we are representing a matchup between opponents as a matchup between their stats.
The model knowns nothing else about the players other than their stats. It determines how much each stat contributes to winning, then
compares the two "players" stats.

############################################################################################################
4: (Optional but I want to do it) After the model is complete, I want to host a website that visualizes a tournament simulation

It doesn't have to look super pretty but it would make this a nice, presentable project that combines several facets of software development.
Maybe I can even spin up a database, store results, etc.
